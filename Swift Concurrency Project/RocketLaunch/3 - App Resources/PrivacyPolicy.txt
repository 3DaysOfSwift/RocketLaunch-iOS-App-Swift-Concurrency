RocketLaunch Privacy Policy
Updated 13 September 2026

RocketLaunch is provided by 3 Days of Swift. It does not require an account and includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Information stored on your device
Your chosen colour theme and reminder records are saved locally. Downloaded launch schedules and the schedule-change history are held in memory during the app session. Theme choices and reminder records are not sent to the launch providers by this app.

Launch data requests
The app connects directly to RocketLaunch.Live, The Space Devs’ Launch Library and the community SpaceX API to retrieve public launch information. These services receive your IP address and the technical information needed to process the request, and may keep server logs under their own policies. The community SpaceX API is not an official SpaceX service.

Reminders
If you choose to set a reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification through iOS. No reminder push server is used. You can remove reminders in the app and change notification permission in iOS Settings. Schedule changes are checked when the app refreshes, not by background monitoring.

External links
Opening a provider, launch-information or watch link connects you to that external service. Its privacy practices apply to your visit.

Your choices
You can use the app without enabling notifications. Remove individual reminders in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; device backups are managed separately by your Apple settings. The app does not offer an account or maintain an account profile to delete.

Questions
